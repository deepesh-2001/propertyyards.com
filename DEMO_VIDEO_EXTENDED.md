## Technical Production Specifications:

### **Visual Requirements:**
- **Resolution:** 4K (3840x2160) for maximum quality
- **Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 (standard) with 9:16 (vertical) versions for social media
- **Color Scheme:** Professional blue, white, gold accents with Indian cultural elements
- **Typography:** Clean, modern fonts with multilingual support

### **Background Videos:**
- **Luxury Properties:** Drone footage of premium Indian real estate
- **Architecture:** Modern and traditional Indian buildings
- **Cityscapes:** Mumbai, Delhi, Bangalore, Hyderabad skylines
- **Interiors:** High-end home interiors and amenities
- **Cultural Elements:** Traditional motifs with modern design

### **Audio Requirements:**
- **Primary Voiceover:** Professional English voice (clear, confident, warm)
- **Multilingual Dubs:** Professional voice actors for Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Arabic
- **Background Music:** Inspiring orchestral track with Indian elements
- **Sound Effects:** Subtle interaction sounds, transitions
- **Audio Quality:** Studio-grade recording and mixing

### **Subtitle & Caption Requirements:**
- **Languages:** English, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Arabic, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Marathi, Gujarati, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi
- **Format:** Closed captions with timing synchronization
- **Style:** Clean, readable fonts with background for visibility
- **Translation:** Professional translation services for accuracy

### **Animation & Graphics:**
- **Logo Animations:** Elegant PropertyYards branding
- **UI Demonstrations:** Smooth screen recordings with callouts
- **Data Visualizations:** Animated statistics and charts
- **Transitions:** Professional wipes and fades
- **Text Overlays:** Key feature highlights with icons

### **Distribution Strategy:**
- **Primary:** YouTube (4K version)
- **Social Media:** Instagram Reels, Facebook Video, LinkedIn
- **Website:** Homepage hero video
- **Mobile App:** Onboarding tutorial
- **Email Marketing:** Embedded video player

### **Target Audience Segments:**
- **Primary:** Property buyers and sellers in India
- **Secondary:** NRI investors and international buyers
- **Tertiary:** Real estate agents and developers
- **Quaternary:** First-time home buyers and young professionals

### **Key Performance Indicators:**
- **View Duration:** Aim for 70%+ completion rate
- **Engagement:** Likes, comments, shares, saves
- **Conversion:** App downloads and website visits
- **Reach:** Target 100K+ views in first month
- **Retention:** Return viewers and brand recall
